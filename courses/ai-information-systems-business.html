Artificial Intelligence and Information Systems for Businesses

Department of Business Administration · University of Macedonia

About the course

The course examines how information systems and Artificial Intelligence support business operations and decision making. Students learn the main types of business information systems, core AI and machine learning concepts, and how organisations adopt them in practice.
