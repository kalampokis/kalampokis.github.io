E-Services in Tourism Businesses

Master in Tourism Management (MTM) · University of Macedonia

About the course

The course examines how tourism businesses use data and digital technologies to design, distribute and improve their services. It opens with what artificial intelligence is changing in the industry and then moves to hands-on work with real booking data: students build Tableau dashboards for indicators such as occupancy, average rate and cancellations, learn how the digital distribution ecosystem works (property management systems, OTAs, metasearch, direct booking) and what each channel costs, analyse visitor behaviour before the booking with Google Analytics, manage online reputation through reviews and social media, and use generative AI tools for content and customer service within the regulatory framework (GDPR, EU AI Act). The emphasis is on the decisions a manager has to make, not on the technical workings of the systems.
