E-Services in Tourism Businesses

Master in Tourism Management (MTM) · University of Macedonia

About the course

The course examines how tourism businesses use digital technologies to design and deliver services. Topics include online booking and distribution channels, e-commerce and digital marketing for tourism, customer reviews and social media, and data-driven decision making in hospitality.
