Advanced Predictive Analytics and Data Mining

MSc in Business Analytics and Data Science (MBADS) · University of Macedonia
Taught by Dr. Evangelos Kalampokis in weeks 7–12

About the course

The course covers advanced methods for building, evaluating and interpreting predictive models on business data. Topics include ensemble methods such as random forests and gradient boosting, model tuning and validation, and data mining techniques for extracting patterns from large datasets.
