MSc in Business Analytics and Data Science · University of Macedonia
Fall 2026

About the course

The course introduces methods for collecting, processing and analysing data from the Web and from unstructured text. Students work hands-on in Python with techniques such as regular expressions, text preprocessing and data wrangling with pandas, and apply them to real-world analytics problems.
